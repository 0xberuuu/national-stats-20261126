Population 2025

Nusantara's population grows by about 0.5 million people a year. A third of the population is under 25, and 58.3% live in towns and cities.

Prices and inflation 2025

Consumer prices rose 3.1% on average in 2025, inside the 2–4% target band for the second year running. Food prices rose fastest, at 3.8%.

Methodology and about

How these statistics are made, and what this site is.

A fictional country. The Republic of Nusantara does not exist. Every figure on this site is invented for demonstration, and no real statistics office's brand, text or images are used.
